# Generative AI I

Notes following MIS 285N (Generative AI I) at UT Austin, taught by
Constantine Caramanis, Spring 2026.

Coverage runs from neural network and CNN fundamentals through logistic
regression and transfer learning, then embeddings and self-supervised
pretraining (word2vec, BERT, masked language modeling), retrieval (bi-encoders,
cross-encoders, late interaction), and multimodal contrastive models (CLIP,
zero-shot classification), ending with adversarial attacks.

The last two sections are simple worked examples and
practice questions.
